01 / ABOUT

About me

I am an MPhil student at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, where I began in September 2026. My current research focuses on MPAS-URBAN. My background spans physical oceanography, atmospheric science and applied mathematics.

My research at the University of Edinburgh focused on super-resolution of ocean turbulence using the quasigeostrophic model pyqg and convolutional neural networks. Previously, I studied Arctic mesoscale eddies and the drivers of tropical cyclone variability in the Arabian Sea.

Python · MATLAB · Fortran · GMT · HYCOM · pyqg · CNNs

02 / RESEARCH

Research interests

Connecting scientific questions, methods and discovery.

01

MPAS-URBAN · Current research

Ongoing MPhil research at The Hong Kong University of Science and Technology, since September 2026.

02

Ocean turbulence & machine learning

Super-resolution of ocean mesoscale turbulence with pyqg simulations and convolutional neural networks. Research project at the University of Edinburgh, 2024–2026.

03

Mesoscale eddies in the Arctic Ocean

Three-dimensional structure of a subsurface anticyclonic eddy in the Canada Basin and seasonal variability of Arctic eddies, using HYCOM and in-situ observations.

04

Tropical cyclones & climate variability

Drivers of Arabian Sea tropical cyclone variability, using IBTrACS tracks and correlation analysis with climate indices including ENSO and the Indian Ocean Dipole.
